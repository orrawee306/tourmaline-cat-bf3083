How to Collect Your Ticket

Collection period
10 Oct – 8 Nov 2026. After that, collect on-site at the venue on your show day only.
Self pick-up
Show your ID card or passport (matching the number used at checkout) at any of the 11 outlets below.
By post
Tickets are mailed out during the collection period. If delivery fails, fall back to on-site pickup on show day.
On-site (show day)
Available only for that day's show — a Day 1 ticket can't be collected as a Day 2 fallback or vice versa.

If you paid by credit / debit card

Collecting it yourself, bring:

  • Your ThaiTicketMajor order confirmation
  • Your ID card / passport (the cardholder's)
  • The physical credit or debit card used to pay (or your ID, if you paid via K‑Web Shopping Card)
  • If the TTM login isn't the cardholder, also bring the account holder's ID

Sending someone else to collect on your behalf? They'll need:

  • The order confirmation
  • A signed authorization letter naming the last 4 digits of your card
  • A signed copy of your ID/passport (the cardholder's)
  • A signed copy of their own ID/passport
Good to know: one ID/passport = one purchase per show, up to 6 tickets per show (12 across both nights). Once a purchase is complete, seats can't be changed, exchanged, or refunded except through the venue's official cancellation window — see your order confirmation for details.

Public transport

🚇

MRT Blue Line — Phahon Yothin Most convenient

Take Exit 5 — it leads straight to the front of Union Mall. This is the route Union Mall itself recommends.

🚆

BTS Sukhumvit Line — Ha Yaek Lat Phrao

Take Exit 2, then walk up onto the MRT skywalk bridge to reach the mall — about 400 metres.

Coming from Don Mueang Airport (DMK)

🚌

Step 1 — Airport shuttle bus to BTS Mo Chit

Take bus A1 (Don Mueang → BTS Mo Chit) or A2 (Don Mueang → Victory Monument). Board at Terminal 1 (International), Floor 1, Gate 6, or Terminal 2 (Domestic), Floor 2, Gate 12/14.

🚉

Step 2 — Get off at the BTS Mo Chit stop

Then continue one stop by BTS or MRT into Union Mall using the routes on the left — MRT Phahon Yothin Exit 5 is the most convenient.

Driving & other options

🚗

Self-drive / parking

From Lat Phrao Road, take the Ha Yaek (five-junction) overpass and keep to the left lane, then turn left straight into Union Mall. Multi-storey parking is available but fills up fast on show day — arrive early or use the MRT if you can.

🚕

Taxi / Grab

Roughly a 10-minute ride from the Siam/BTS Mo Chit area outside rush hour — build in extra time for event-night traffic near the venue.

🤝

Split the ride

Heading in with a Grab or car and have spare seats? Post it on the Travel Buddy tab so other fans can join you and split the fare.
